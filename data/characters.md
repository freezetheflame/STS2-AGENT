## 1. The Ironclad

- **Starting HP:** 80
- **Unique Card Pool:** 86 cards
- **Key Mechanics / Keywords:** Strength, Self-Damage, Vulnerability, Exhaust, Plating, Demon Form, Hemokinesis
- **Playstyle Description:**  
  Returning in Slay the Spire 2 as the master of martial prowess and demonic pacts. Enhanced Vulnerability synergies, deeper Self-Damage scaling, and advanced Exhaust interactions raise his tactical ceiling higher than ever. Core self-damage synergies preserved — trade HP for raw power through Hemokinesis and Blood for Blood. Strength-scaling berserker with refreshed card pool. Archetypes include Juggernaut (Plating/Armor), Master of Strikes, Aggressive Recycler, Demon Strength Scaling, Exhaust Recursion, and Vulnerability Executioner.

## 2. The Silent

- **Starting HP:** 70
- **Unique Card Pool:** 86 cards
- **Key Mechanics / Keywords:** Sly, Poison, Shivs, Discard, Hand Management
- **Playstyle Description:**  
  Returning in Slay the Spire 2 as a deadly acrobat specializing in small attacks and toxins. The new Sly keyword transforms discard-heavy decks — cards with Sly can be played traditionally for their Energy cost, or discarded to trigger their effect automatically for 0 Energy. Evolved Shiv synergies make her more explosive than ever. Poison specialist with new discard-combo archetypes. Archetypes include Sly Discard Engine, Shiv Storm, Poison Engine, and Hand Management.

- **Key Mechanics / Keywords:** Orbs (Lightning, Frost, Dark, Plasma, Glass), Focus, Channel, Evoke, Slot Management

## 4. Necrobinder

- **Starting HP:** 66
- **Unique Card Pool:** 86 cards
- **Key Mechanics / Keywords:** Osty (Companion), Doom, Souls, Card Transform, Ethereal, Summon, Proxy Combat
- **Playstyle Description:**  
  A master of the macabre in Slay the Spire 2, wielding proxy combat through her companion Osty — a skeletal companion who attacks, tanks damage, and scales via Summon. Stack Doom to instantly execute enemies when the threshold is met, and generate Souls for burst card draw. Soul token cycling enables explosive velocity turns. Archetypes include Osty Wall, Doom Rush, Soul Velocity, and Self-Doom Burst. The most mechanically complex character in the franchise.

## 5. The Regent

- **Starting HP:** 75
- **Unique Card Pool:** 87 cards
- **Key Mechanics / Keywords:** Stars, Forge, Sovereign Blade, Minions, Colorless, Transform, Card Transformation
- **Playstyle Description:**  
  A sophisticated and mysterious figure who manages Stars as a persistent secondary resource (no cap, carries over between turns). Focuses on resource management, card transformation, and the Forge mechanic to scale the Sovereign Blade — a scaling weapon that grows stronger each time you Forge. Transform cards into Minions and leverage unique Colorless card synergies. Slow-burn playstyle: requires setup to generate Stars and Forge the Blade, then cash in for massive turns. High difficulty — one of STS2's most demanding characters. Archetypes include Star Economy, Minion Swarm, Forge Scaling, and Colorless Synergy.
